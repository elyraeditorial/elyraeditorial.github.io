The Contestant Campaign Kit

You are one of the faces in the running for the cover. The public decides who gets it. This is exactly how you win the votes — every step, in order, nothing left for you to guess.

01 — The Format

Three rounds. Votes reset each time.

Read the last line of this table twice. It is the single thing most contestants get wrong.

RoundWhat happens
Open RoundEveryone competes. The top vote-getters advance to the Semi-Finals.
Semi-FinalsEvery score returns to zero. The field narrows again to the Finalists.
FinaleZero again. One winner takes the cover of the Power Issue.
What this means for you A commanding lead in the Open Round is worth nothing on the first morning of the Semi-Finals. You are not running one campaign. You are running three. Contestants lose this by celebrating a win and going quiet for a week.
02 — Do This First

Your first 30 minutes

Finish all six before you post anything. If your link isn't working, every share you send is wasted.

1

Find your voting link and test it

  1. Open elyraeditorial.com and go to the competition page.
  2. Find your own name and photo in the contestant list.
  3. Copy the link that opens your profile.
  4. Send that link to yourself in a text message.
  5. Open it from your phone. Confirm your face and name appear, and confirm the vote button works.
WhyIf the link is broken, every text, story and post you send after this points at nothing. Test before you promote — not after.
2

Put the link in your bio

  1. Open Instagram and tap your profile picture in the bottom right corner.
  2. Tap Edit profile.
  3. Tap Links, then Add external link.
  4. Paste your voting link. In the title field type: VOTE FOR ME — Cover Star.
  5. Tap Done, then go back to your profile and tap the link yourself to confirm it opens.
  6. Repeat on TikTok: Edit profile → Website → paste → save.
WhyMost platforms hide or strip links inside captions. The bio link is the one place a tap reliably works, so every post you make will point people there.
3

Add the ask to your bio text

  1. Still in Edit profile, tap into your bio text.
  2. Add a new line at the very bottom: 🖤 Cover Star finalist — vote below ↓
  3. Save.
WhyA link with no instruction gets ignored. The arrow tells a stranger what to do in under a second.
4

Record your announcement video

  1. Stand facing a window, with the light on your face — never behind you.
  2. Hold the phone at eye level, slightly above. Not below your chin.
  3. Film vertical. Look at the lens, not at your own face on the screen.
  4. Say Script A from the next section. Say your own full name in it.
  5. Keep it under 12 seconds. Record it three times and keep the most natural take.
WhyPeople vote for a person, not a poster. A plain phone video of you asking sincerely outperforms a polished graphic almost every time.
5

Post it and pin it to the top of your profile

  1. Post the video as a Reel with the Launch caption from below.
  2. Once it's live, tap the three dots on the post.
  3. Tap Pin to your profile.
WhyPinning means every single new person who lands on your profile for the rest of the competition sees the ask first, without scrolling.
6

Text twenty people individually

  1. Open your messages and scroll your recent contacts.
  2. Write down twenty names. Family, close friends, old colleagues, group chats.
  3. Message each one separately. Use their name. Never send a group blast.
  4. Send: "Hey [name] — I'm competing for a magazine cover and it's decided by public votes. Takes 5 seconds, would mean a lot: [link]"
  5. Tick them off your list as you go.
WhyThis is the highest-converting thing in the entire kit. A personal message gets a vote roughly one time in two. A story to two thousand followers rarely does better than one in a hundred.
03 — Say This

Five scripts, ten seconds each

Your voice, your face, straight to camera. Say your own name once so people can find you on the voting page.

Script A · Launch

I'm [your name], and I'm competing for the cover of ELYRA Editorial's Power Issue. This is decided by votes. One tap, link in my bio. Let's get me on that cover.

Use the day voting opens.
Script B · Warm

Something big is happening. I'm in the running for a magazine cover, and votes decide it. It takes five seconds. Link in my bio — go vote for me. Please.

Use mid-round, around day 3.
Script C · Urgent

Voting closes soon and I'm right there. Every single vote counts in this round. Link in my bio, takes five seconds. Don't let me miss this one.

Use in the final 48 hours of any round.
Script D · Round reset

I made it to the next round — thank you. But the votes reset to zero, so I need you one more time. Same link, five seconds. Let's finish this.

Record fresh on day one of the Semi-Finals and again for the Finale.
Script E · Thank you

We did it. I'm through to the next round because of you. I'm not done asking, but right now I just want to say thank you. Genuinely.

Post within hours of each round closing.

How to record it properly

  1. Face a window. Daylight on your face beats any ring light.
  2. Phone at eye level or just above. Never angled up from your chest.
  3. Quiet room. No music behind you — it competes with your voice.
  4. Look into the lens. Not at yourself on screen.
  5. Add captions in the app. Most people watch with the sound off.
  6. Three takes, keep the warmest one. Don't chase perfect — chase sincere.

05 — Daily Stories

Seven frames to rotate

Two or three a day while a round is live. Rotate them, or it reads as spam and people mute you.

1

Selfie with a link sticker

Text on frame: "Voting is open. One tap." Add the link sticker so it's tappable — don't make people go find your bio.

2

Screenshot of your competition profile

Seeing the real page proves it's legitimate. Circle your own name with the draw tool.

3

Behind the scenes

Getting ready, a shoot, your ordinary day. Put "and voting's still open" as a small line at the bottom, not the headline.

4

Countdown sticker

Set it to the exact hour the round closes. It reappears in people's feeds as the deadline approaches.

5

Repost a supporter

When someone shares your link, reshare it. A recommendation from them carries further than another ask from you.

6

Question sticker

"Ask me anything about the competition." Answer in follow-up frames, link sticker on each one.

7

End-of-round recap

Where you landed, who to thank, what's coming next. This one earns you the right to ask again.

07 — The Difference

What actually moves votes

  • One-to-one beats one-to-many. Twenty personal texts will out-convert a story seen by two thousand people. This is the whole game.
  • Pin the post. Every new visitor should hit the ask before they scroll.
  • Get other people posting. Their followers are an audience you can't reach yourself.
  • Say your name out loud. Supporters arrive at a page of faces and leave without voting if they can't find you.
  • Post when your people are awake. Check your own analytics. Ignore general advice, including this sentence.
  • Finish strong. Most contestants fade in the last 48 hours. That's precisely when the round is decided.
08 — Non-Negotiable

The rules

Breaking any of these removes you from the competition. No warnings, no appeals.

  • No bought votes, bots, or vote-farming services. Purchased or automated votes are grounds for immediate disqualification.
  • No fake or duplicate accounts. One person, one vote.
  • No campaigning against other contestants. Compete for yourself. Disparaging another entrant is grounds for removal.
  • No announcing results before we do. Wait for the official publication of each round.
  • Tag @elyraeditorial so your posts can be found and reshared by the House.
